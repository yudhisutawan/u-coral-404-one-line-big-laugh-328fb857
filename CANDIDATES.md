## Winner

85 characters. Why it wins: a real joke with a sting (criterion 1); the turn is
invisible until the final word — "fix the leak" promises home repair, the reply
re-frames it as the marriage (criterion 2); 85 characters, every word earns its
place (criterion 3); original wording, not a stock joke (criterion 4); the
marriage callback is the kind of line repeated to a friend the same day
(criterion 5). Small text also helps the tie-break (fewer bytes wins exact ties).
